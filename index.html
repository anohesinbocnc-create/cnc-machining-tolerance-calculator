Scope & limitations

ISO 2768 applies to non-fit dimensions without individual tolerance indications — it does not apply to mating features (use ISO 286 hole/shaft fits instead), to features with individual tolerances on the drawing, or to edges, chamfers and radii unless stated. For bearing bores, seal grooves and similar functional features, tolerance the drawing explicitly.
